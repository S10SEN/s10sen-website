About Me

My professional life centers around facilities management. I entered the field as a welder and worked my way into enterprise-level program management and facilities planning. Along the way, I earned a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and a master's degree in Public Administration with a specialization in Organizational Leadership.

Outside of my professional work, I have a strong interest in computer science and technology. I enjoy exploring software at all levels, from the systems that run our computers to the applications we interact with every day, as well as networking, artificial intelligence, and data science.

About s10sen.com

This site exists to serve a few different purposes: a place for me to experiment and learn, a way to showcase my skills and projects, and an opportunity for people to learn more about who I am.

The site will document my work with homelabbing and networking, while also serving as a place to develop and demonstrate my web development skills with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and eventually technologies such as React and full-stack development. I also plan to showcase projects using other programming languages, including C and Python, as the site continues to grow.

What I'm Interested In

My interests in computer science tend to center around understanding how things work beneath the surface. I am particularly interested in low-level programming, Linux, operating systems, and kernel development. I enjoy learning how software interacts with hardware and how the different layers of a computer system work together.

I am also interested in networking, self-hosting, and homelabbing, which give me an opportunity to apply many of these concepts in a practical environment. More broadly, I enjoy building things, experimenting with new technologies, and developing a deeper understanding of the systems I use every day.
